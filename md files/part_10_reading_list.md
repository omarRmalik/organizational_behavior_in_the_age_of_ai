## Overview

The course combines a core textbook with a curated set of classic and contemporary research articles. Contemporary readings come mainly from the *Journal of Organizational Behavior*, *Academy of Management Journal*, *Administrative Science Quarterly*, *Journal of Management Studies*, and *Personnel Psychology*.

Articles marked **[Open Access]** are published under a Creative Commons license and can be read free of charge by anyone. Other articles are available through Kettering University library access via the DOI link. Articles listed as "advance online publication" have been published online but not yet assigned to a journal issue.

## Weekly Readings and the OB Theory Update

For each pair of readings, students should ask:

1. What behavior does the classic theory explain?
2. What does it assume about who knows and who decides?
3. What does the AI study find?
4. Which assumption does the AI study support, challenge, or change?
5. How would the theory look if it were rewritten for workplaces where people and AI know and decide together?

These questions prepare students for the OB Theory Update in each weekly reflection.
